The BYU pages expose expected sample answers and grading policies online. They
do not supply the detailed 10-point rubrics or the full Python answer keys used
here. `rubrics/` and `reference_answers/` are original demo material created for
this repository; **do not describe them as official BYU/Berkeley rubrics or
downloaded staff solutions**. In particular, product(n=0), bounded domains and
ascending accumulation order are local clarifications.

The 60 submissions are generated combinations of deliberately authored solution
strategies and mistakes, labeled T001–T030. They are not a statistical sample of
students, independent human answers, or a benchmark proving model accuracy.
Do not use algorithm similarity or intentional duplicates as plagiarism labels.
Links retain attribution; original web pages, staff code and course archives
are not redistributed wholesale. There is no university endorsement.
